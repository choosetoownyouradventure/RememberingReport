ISABELL · CHOOSE TO OWN YOUR ADVENTURE™

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Choose to Own Your Adventure™ (“CtOYA,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information you provide when you use the Remember Your Story experience, request a Story Page, continue into a deeper assessment, or book a conversation with a Story Guide.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the experience, we may collect your first name, email address, your responses to reflective questions, the resulting story or assessment information generated from those responses, a Story ID, progress/completion information, and information you voluntarily provide when contacting or booking with us.

How we use it

We use this information to save your place, generate and deliver your personalized Story Page, allow you to continue your experience, respond to requests, improve the coaching experience, and—if you choose—to arrange a clarity call or related coaching service.

Technology and service providers

The site may use service providers that process information on our behalf. These currently include GitHub Pages for website hosting, Cloudflare Workers and D1 for application processing and story storage, and EmailJS for transactional email delivery. If you choose to book a call, Google Calendar may process the booking information you submit on its booking page.

Browser storage

To help you continue a story from the same browser, the site may store a Story ID and a private resume credential in your browser’s local storage. Clearing browser data, using a different browser, or changing devices may remove access to that locally stored resume information.

Spiritual and reflective information

Remember Your Story is a reflective spiritual-coaching experience. References to archetypes, Angelic, Starseed, Earth, intuition, spirituality, or similar concepts are presented as interpretive frameworks for reflection. They are not medical, psychological, diagnostic, or scientific determinations.

Story delivery and practitioner copy

Providing an email address and accepting the required story consent lets us save and send your requested Story Page. ISABELL also receives a copy of that email for administering the experience and responding to your requests. A provider accepting an email request does not guarantee inbox delivery.

Optional marketing preference

An additional unchecked choice lets you opt into occasional reflective insights, program information, and invitations. You may receive your requested Story Page without opting into marketing. You can withdraw marketing permission by using the unsubscribe instructions in a promotional message or contacting us at the address below.

Email and communications

If you ask us to save or send your Story Page, we use the email address you provide for that purpose. We will not treat completion of the story as permission for unrelated marketing unless you separately choose to receive it.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the experience, deliver requested communications, secure the service, or comply with legal obligations.

Consent and lead records

For new stories, we record the required consent date and wording version and your separate marketing choice. We may record whether the email service accepted a send request and basic engagement with the story or a requested clarity call when that information is available. Historical records may not contain these consent details. We do not treat a requested Story Page or a BCC copy as permission for unrelated marketing.

Retention

We keep story and contact information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, to maintain the continuity of an experience you chose to save, or as required for legitimate business or legal needs. You may ask us to delete information associated with your Story ID and email address.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide an email address, although the saved-story and email-delivery portions of the experience may then be unavailable. You may also request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided to us, subject to applicable law and reasonable identity verification.

Children

This experience is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 18.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service. No website or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the experience evolves. The date at the top will indicate the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact choosetoownyouradventure@gmail.com. If your request concerns a saved Remembering Story, include the Story ID shown on your result page if you still have it.

Note: This policy is designed to match the current Remember Your Story implementation. If you later add payment processing, advertising pixels, analytics, international data-transfer mechanisms, or additional platforms, the policy should be updated before those features go live.
